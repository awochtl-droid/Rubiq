The 2026 Estate Tax Exemption: $15 Million Per Person, and No More Sunset

The federal estate tax exemption did not get cut in half in 2026. The 2025 tax law raised it to $15 million per person and made it permanent. Here is how the exemption works, why married couples should still file for portability, and what Pennsylvania families still owe.

What changed, and the sunset that didn’t happen

Under the 2017 tax law, the exemption was scheduled to fall by roughly half at the start of 2026, to about $7 million per person. Many families made large gifts in 2024 and 2025 to use the higher amount before it disappeared.

The 2025 tax law replaced that sunset. Starting January 1, 2026, the exemption is $15 million per person, it is indexed for inflation in later years, and there is no expiration date. It can still be changed by a future Congress, but nothing in current law reduces it.

The 2027 amount will be slightly higher once the IRS publishes its annual inflation adjustment, which it typically does in the fall.

How the exemption works

  • One exemption for gifts and estates. Taxable gifts you make during life, the amounts above the annual exclusion, use up the same $15 million. What is left at death shelters your estate. See the gift tax guide.
  • Tax applies only above it. Estate tax is owed on the taxable estate above the remaining exemption, at a top rate of 40%.
  • Spouses and charities come off the top. Everything left to a U.S. citizen spouse or to charity is deductible, so most first deaths in a marriage owe no estate tax at all.
  • The GST exemption is separate. Transfers to grandchildren, or to trusts for them, use a separate $15 million generation-skipping exemption, which must be allocated deliberately.

Portability: why married couples should still file

When the first spouse dies, any exemption they did not use can pass to the survivor. This is called portability, and it lets a couple shelter up to $30 million. It is not automatic: the executor must file a federal estate tax return (Form 706) to elect it, even when no tax is owed. Estates that were not otherwise required to file can generally make the election up to five years after death.

Two limits are worth knowing. The unused amount that passes to the survivor is fixed at the first death and does not grow with inflation, and the GST exemption does not port at all.

A worked example

A married Chester County couple has a $24 million estate. When the first spouse dies, everything passes to the survivor, so no estate tax is due. When the survivor later dies, the estate passes to their children.

Pennsylvania has no estate tax, but it has an inheritance tax

Pennsylvania taxes what heirs receive, at 0% for a surviving spouse, 4.5% for children and other lineal descendants, 12% for siblings, and 15% for most others, with no exemption amount. A modest estate left to children pays the same 4.5% rate as a very large one. See the Pennsylvania inheritance tax guide.

Who still needs estate tax planning

  • Estates above, or growing toward, $15 million or $30 million. A business or real estate portfolio that compounds for 20 years can cross the line even if it is under it today. Our estate tax estimator projects your exposure.
  • Families in states with their own estate tax. Some states, including New York, Massachusetts, and Maryland, tax estates far below the federal exemption. Owning real estate in one of them can create a state filing even for a Pennsylvania resident.
  • Everyone, for income tax. With no federal estate tax to plan around, the bigger lever for most families is the step-up in basis: holding low-basis assets until death can erase capital gains that a lifetime gift would carry over.

Frequently asked questions

What is the estate tax exemption for 2026?

The federal estate and gift tax exemption is $15 million per person in 2026, or $30 million for a married couple who elect portability. Estates below the exemption owe no federal estate tax; amounts above it are taxed at up to 40%.

Did the estate tax exemption sunset in 2026?

No. The exemption had been scheduled to drop by about half at the start of 2026, but the 2025 tax law instead raised it to $15 million per person, indexed it for inflation in later years, and removed the scheduled expiration.

What will the estate tax exemption be in 2027?

The $15 million exemption is indexed for inflation after 2026, so the 2027 amount will be slightly higher. The IRS publishes the official figure in its annual inflation adjustments, typically in the fall.

What is estate tax portability?

Portability lets a surviving spouse use the unused federal estate tax exemption of the first spouse to die. The executor must elect it on a timely filed Form 706, even if no tax is due; estates not otherwise required to file can generally elect within five years of death. The ported amount is not indexed for inflation, and the GST exemption does not port.

Does Pennsylvania have an estate tax?

No. Pennsylvania has no estate tax, but it has an inheritance tax on what heirs receive: 0% for a surviving spouse, 4.5% for children and other lineal descendants, 12% for siblings, and 15% for most other heirs, with no exemption amount.
